Agenda's segments fit one row in a default sidebar, and the Projects heading reads as a collection instead of as one more project.

**Agenda's segments fit one row**
- Three segments in a 300px sidebar wrapped onto two rows, and the second row cost the list its height every time you opened Agenda. **Agenda** and **Upcoming** now keep their icon, their word and their count at every width — they are the two you work from. **Reminders** stands on its bell and its count, and takes its word back the moment the column can afford it: drag the sidebar wider, or open Agenda in the main area.
- The bell is never a mystery. Hover it and Obsidian names it, and a screen reader reads "Reminders" whether or not the word is drawn.
- The pills are four pixels tighter on each side, which is where the room came from. Narrower than about 290px the row still wraps — two rows is the pane telling you it is too narrow to read, and the fix is to widen it.
- On a phone the column is the whole screen, so all three segments keep their words.

**Projects reads as a collection**
- The **Projects** heading in the navigator drew the same folder-with-columns glyph as every project under it, so the heading looked like one more project rather than the thing holding them.
- It is now a stacked pair of folders. The navigator reads in three tiers at a glance: stacked folders for the collection, a folder with branches for a group you made, and the folder with columns for a project itself.
- Only the icon changed. Nothing moved, nothing was renamed, and no note was touched.
