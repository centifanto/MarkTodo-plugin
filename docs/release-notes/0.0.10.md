MarkTodo now follows your Obsidian theme instead of painting its own, the six status colours are fixed and drawn from Flexoki, every view has the same bar with filters and sort on it, and every setting in that bar wears an icon.

**MarkTodo follows your theme**
- The **Theme** and **Mode** settings are gone. Backgrounds, text, borders and controls in MarkTodo's panes are your Obsidian theme's now, in whatever light or dark you are running — there is no MarkTodo look to switch to, and nothing that can fall out of step with the vault around it.
- If you had MarkTodo forced to Light inside a dark vault (or the other way round), that pane now matches the rest of Obsidian. Nothing to do; it just stops being different.
- **Accent stays**, and is now the only appearance colour MarkTodo picks. It colours selection, links and buttons inside MarkTodo's panes.

**Flexoki**
- The accent presets are now [Flexoki](https://stephango.com/flexoki) — an inky palette by Steph Ango, Obsidian's own CEO, built for reading on screens: muted and slightly warm rather than saturated, and calibrated per hue for light and dark.
- Your existing choice carries over: **Amber becomes Yellow, Rose becomes Red, Graphite becomes Base.** Match Obsidian's accent still does exactly that.

**The six status colours are fixed**
- Backlog neutral, Warming yellow, Doing blue, Blocked red, Paused purple, Done green — the same six in every vault, on every device.
- **This is a change:** Doing and Done used to take *your* accent, and Paused shifted its hue to avoid clashing with it. A status heading already means one thing in every vault, and now it looks the same in every vault too — so a board reads the same over someone's shoulder as it does on your own screen.

**Filters and sort on every view**
- **Projects can be filtered.** A project's list and its board now offer tag, priority and managed filters. They share one setting, so narrowing a project in the dashboard narrows its board too.
- **Today has the full bar**: filters, sort *and* grouping, each remembered per segment. The old sort-and-group button in Today's header is gone — the bar replaces it.
- **List, Kanban and Source** now sit in the bar on every surface. A project's board used to draw the switch on a row of its own above the bar.
- The collapsed line names everything it is doing, including the layout and the grouping: **List | All | 0 filters | Manual | Status**.

**Every setting wears an icon**
- The focus segments, Today's four tabs, every sort and every grouping option now carry a glyph, and the collapsed summary is drawn with those icons instead of printed as one grey sentence.
- Sort and Group show the icon of the *setting*, not of the current value, so a pill reading "Date" can never leave you wondering whether a calendar meant sorted by date or grouped by date.

**The bar reads properly now**
- One type scale: the summary line matches your todo titles, chips and dropdowns sit a step under it, and only the small captions above each group are smaller. The summary spans the full width rather than floating as a short pill.
- Filters, Sort and Group always stack, one section per row, instead of rearranging into columns partway through a resize. Everything inside them lines up on one grid.
- **Switching focus no longer makes the bar jump.** A focus with fewer todos stopped the list scrolling, which removed its scrollbar, which resized the pane and reflowed the whole bar. The scrollbar gutter is now reserved either way.
- All four filter controls are one size and one height. Project and tag are text pickers and priority and managed are dropdowns, and they had been rendering at two different sizes on adjacent rows.

**Smaller things**
- **Settings shows the plugin version**, under a new **About** section with the contact links.
- New installs open the dashboard in the **left sidebar**. If you have already set this, your choice is kept.
