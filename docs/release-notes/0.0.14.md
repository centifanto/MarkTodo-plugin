The New todo dialog is fixed on phones: the priority chips fit the row again, an empty due date is a field you can see, and the dialog opens where the todo editor opens.

**Priority chips fit the row**
- On a phone, the four priority chips in **New todo** were each drawn a twelfth of the dialog wide — **Urge**, **Hig**, **Low**, and then **None** on a second row, hanging off the left edge of the dialog. The todo editor's own priority row was always fine; only capture was affected.
- The row now asks for its columns by width rather than by count. Four chips fit a normal phone, and a narrower one drops to three and wraps to a second row — the labels are never shaved down to make them fit.

**An empty due date is a field you can see**
- With no date set, **Due** drew nothing at all on a phone. Obsidian gives an input its fill and leaves the edges to a border that touch devices set to zero, and inside a dialog that fill is the dialog's own background — so the row read as a gap beside a **Destination** dropdown that does have a surface. Every new todo starts without a due date, so this was every new todo.
- Due now draws its own edge, and stands at the same height as the dropdowns above it, on a phone and on a desktop.
- The **✕** beside it appears when there is a date to clear, and not before. This is true in the todo editor too.

**New todo opens where the editor opens**
- The dialog's title sat a line lower than the todo editor's title, on a band of empty space, because Obsidian gives every heading a margin above it and the editor's title is not a heading. The two dialogs now start at the same place.

**The capture hint fits the screen**
- The hint after "What needs doing?" was cut off mid-word on a phone, which is worse than not showing it. A phone now gets the question alone.
- On a keyboard the hint names `@high` rather than the `@ph` shorthand. Both still work, along with `@urgent`, `@low`, `@pu` and `@pl` — `@high` is the one MarkTodo writes back into your note, so it is the one worth learning.
